# Privacy Policy for Spinly

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Spinly respects your privacy.

## Data Storage and Use

Spinly does not automatically transmit your personal data to the developer.

Your workouts, recorded rides, app settings, and any athlete profile information you provide are stored locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, this information may include your date of birth, sex, weight, cycling activity level, FTP, maximum heart rate, and measurements recorded during your rides.

Spinly uses this information on your device to provide workout targets, estimates, and ride statistics. No account is required.

## Bluetooth Devices

Spinly uses Bluetooth to connect to compatible bikes, power meters, and heart rate monitors.

Measurements received from these devices, such as power, cadence, and heart rate, are used to display and record your rides. These measurements are not automatically sent to the developer.

You can manage Bluetooth permission in your device's Settings.

## Third-Party Services

Spinly does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

Any payments made through the App Store are processed by Apple. Spinly does not receive or store your payment information.

## Data Export, Import, and Backups

Spinly allows you to export, import, back up, and share data at your request. Depending on the export, files may contain workouts, ride measurements, athlete profile information, or app settings.

You choose where exported files are saved and with whom they are shared. If you choose a cloud storage provider or another app, that service's privacy policy applies.

App data may also be included in device backups, depending on your device and iCloud settings. The developer does not have access to those backups.

## Diagnostics and Support

Spinly keeps a limited local diagnostic log to help investigate connection and sensor problems. Diagnostic files may include device names and identifiers, hardware details, measurements, and app and operating system versions.

Diagnostic information is not uploaded automatically. You can choose to export and share it when requesting support.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems.

## Managing Your Data

You can delete saved rides and workouts within the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored app data.

Exported files, shared copies, and existing backups must be managed separately in the locations where they are stored.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Spinly's functionality changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
